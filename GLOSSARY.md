# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to students. This glossary covers the language around buying places on a course and getting money back.

## Language

### Courses

**Self-Paced Course**:
A course students work through on their own, at any time.

**Cohort Course**:
A course that also has live Office Hours for a defined period after it opens. Students can still take it at their own pace, and it becomes a Self-Paced Course once the live period ends.
_Avoid_: Live course, Bootcamp

**Office Hours**:
The live sessions that make a course a Cohort Course.

**Intro Module**:
The first module of every course: setup and orientation lessons that are free for anyone to watch, before or without buying.
_Avoid_: Preview, Free module, Intro section

**Started Lesson**:
A lesson outside the Intro Module whose video a Student has played at all. It belongs to the Student on that course, whichever Seat they were in.

**Completed Lesson**:
A lesson outside the Intro Module whose video a Student has watched to the end. Like a Started Lesson, it follows the Student between Seats on the same course.
_Avoid_: Finished, Watched (ambiguous between started and completed)

### Purchasing

**Purchaser**:
The person who paid for one or more Seats on a course. Refunds always go back to them.
_Avoid_: Buyer, Customer, Owner

**Individual Purchase**:
A purchase of one Seat, which the Purchaser claims for themselves. They may Transfer it once, and it can never be returned.

**Transfer**:
An Individual Purchaser giving their Seat to one other person, which ends the Purchaser's own access. Allowed once per Individual Purchase.
_Avoid_: Gift, Reassign

**Team Purchase**:
A purchase of several Seats that the Purchaser hands out to Students and can take back.
_Avoid_: Licence, Bulk purchase

**Seat**:
One place on a course, paid for by a Purchaser. A Seat is unclaimed, claimed, or locked. Seats never expire.
_Avoid_: Coupon, Licence, Enrollment (for the paid place)

**Student**:
The person who has claimed a Seat and takes the course. The same person can't hold two Seats on the same course.
_Avoid_: Learner, Seat Holder, User (in domain talk)

**Claimed Seat**:
A Seat with a Student's email address on it.

**Unclaimed Seat**:
A Seat from a Team Purchase with no Student on it, waiting for the Purchaser to give it out or cancel it.
_Avoid_: Open Seat, Available Seat, Vacant Seat

**Locked Seat**:
A Claimed Seat whose Student has passed the Refund Policy's watch limits. It belongs to that Student for good: it can't be returned, transferred, or refunded.
_Avoid_: Used Seat

**Returned Seat**:
A Seat from a Team Purchase that its Student gave back, or the Purchaser took back, making it an Unclaimed Seat again. No money moves. Only Team Purchase Seats can be returned, and never once Locked.
_Avoid_: Unseat, Vacate, Release

**Refunded Seat**:
A Seat that has been cancelled for a Refund. It is kept for history but no longer gives anyone access.
_Avoid_: Deleted Seat, Cancelled Seat

### Refunds

**Refund**:
The money returned to a Purchaser when Seats are cancelled. It records the reason given. A Refund exists only because a cancellation happened; there is no separate cancellation record.
_Avoid_: Refund Request, Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**In-Progress Refund**:
A Refund where the money hasn't been confirmed as sent yet.
_Avoid_: Pending Refund, Pending

**Completed Refund**:
A Refund where the money has been confirmed as sent back to the Purchaser.
_Avoid_: Issued, Paid out, Chargeback

**Failed Refund**:
A Refund where the money still couldn't be sent after retrying. The team is alerted, and the Seat stays a Refunded Seat.

### Eligibility

**Refund Policy**:
The rules that decide whether a Seat can be cancelled for a Refund: its Student must have fewer than 4 Completed Lessons and fewer than 8 Started Lessons on the course. There is no time limit. A Seat is always judged by the Refund Policy in force when it was bought, not the current one.
_Avoid_: Refund rules, Refund terms

**Refund Eligibility**:
Whether a Seat can be cancelled for a Refund right now under its Refund Policy. Cadence decides this itself; nobody on the team approves Refunds. The one exception: when a Team Purchase Seat is given to a Student who already holds an Individual Purchase Seat on that course, the Individual Seat is refunded regardless, and the team Seat becomes a Locked Seat.
_Avoid_: Approval

**Ineligible for Refund**:
A Seat that can no longer be cancelled for a Refund, which in practice means a Locked Seat. The Student or Purchaser is told why, and has no way to cancel; any exception goes through contacting the team directly.
_Avoid_: Declined, Rejected
