# Future

Ideas we've deliberately deferred. Not commitments.

## Per-course Refund Policy

Today one Refund Policy applies to every course. We may want each course to carry its own terms (different windows, different watch limits).

## Partial Refunds

Refunds are all-or-nothing for now. Later we may refund part of a purchase, for example in proportion to how much of the course was left unwatched, while keeping access to what was watched and to the course community.

## Cohort Courses

Name agreed (see GLOSSARY.md), not built. Today every course is self-paced.

## Detecting outros

A Completed Lesson currently means watched to the very end. Many students stop when the outro starts. If we can automatically detect where each video's outro begins, count reaching it as completing the lesson.

## Seat Blocks

Investigate showing a Purchaser's Seats on a course as "Seat Blocks", one per Team Purchase, each with its own expiration date, while still presenting a single seat map for the course.

## Refunds long after purchase

Seats never expire and have no refund deadline, so a Refund may be requested years after purchase. The original card may be closed or changed by then, which will produce Failed Refunds. Work out how we refund in that case.

## Watch counts and multiple email addresses

Started and Completed Lessons follow the Student, keyed by email address. A Student with several email addresses could be put into Seats under each of them and reset their counts each time. Similarly, an Individual Purchaser can watch up to the limit, Transfer, and the new Student can watch up to the limit again before cancelling. Decide whether this needs closing.
