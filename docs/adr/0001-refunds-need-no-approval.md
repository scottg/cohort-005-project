# Refunds need no team approval

Cadence decides Refund Eligibility itself from the Refund Policy, and only shows a cancel option on a Seat that is eligible. So every cancellation produces a Refund straight away, with no review step and no "declined" outcome. We chose this over hand-reviewed requests because the courses are digital, so a Refund costs us little, and taking the team out of routine refunds removes both workload and hesitation at purchase. Anyone who is Ineligible for Refund but has mitigating circumstances contacts the team directly, outside the system.
